🔍 Architectural Scalability Analysis

1. Hyper's Flawless 100k & 200k Scalability

Hyper delivered an exceptional 35,409 RPS at 100,000 pure TCP connections and 36,159 RPS at 200,000 concurrent requests with zero connection drops. Its zero-copy HTTP state machine seamlessly handles 100k+ open descriptors.

2. Monoio (io_uring) Stability

Monoio reliably sustained 60,000 and 100,000 pure TCP connections with 24.8k–25.4k RPS (99.89%+ success rate). The Linux io_uring submission queue avoids epoll overhead at massive connection density.

3. Traefik Collapses at 60k

At 60,000 pure TCP connections, Traefik's Go runtime collapsed under goroutine scheduler and GC pressure, dropping 93.9% of all incoming requests (1.75 Million connection refusals).
